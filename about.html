About Starting Point

A social enterprise investing in people — dignified work, training, and opportunity for newcomers to Canada and community members who need it.

Our Story

How we started

Starting Point Ventures Inc. is part of a small group of companies. It was created by Dr. Omer Mallhi, owner of Paradise Dental Care in Paradise, NL, to reinvest directly into the community.

The company takes no profit out. Every dollar it earns goes back into training and employment, particularly for newcomers to Canada building a life here.
