Our Team

Dr. Omer Mallhi

Director and Founder

Dr. Omer Mallhi founded Starting Point Ventures Inc. to reinvest directly into the community that became his home. He's also the owner of Paradise Dental Care in Paradise, NL, where he's practiced advanced restorative and family dentistry for nearly two decades. Every dollar Starting Point earns stays in the business — reinvested into training and employment, not taken out as profit.
